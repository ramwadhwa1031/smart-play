About Smart Start

A second home where childhood blossoms

Meet the school, the vision and the people behind Smart Start – The Premium School.

Our story

A second home, built with love

Smart Start – The Premium School began with a simple belief: when you leave your little one at our door, your world becomes our responsibility.

We are a premium pre-school in Delhi built around the Multiple Intelligence concept. Our vision was never just to teach A-B-C. It was to create a place where children are nourished in mind and heart, where they learn how to hold confidence, not just a pencil.

Every child gets personal love, personal attention and personal care, and every parent becomes a partner in the journey.

Our vision

Nurture happy, confident & curious learners

At Smart Start The Premium School, our vision is to create a second home for your child. We believe that the first 5 years are not just about learning A-B-C, but about building a beautiful human being. Our vision is to provide a premium, safe, and loving environment where every child feels confident, curious, and cared for. We don't just prepare children for school, we prepare them for life, with strong values, good manners, clear communication, and a happy heart.

We dream of a place where learning is not a burden, but a joyful celebration.

Smart Start – Where Childhood Blossoms & Futures Begin.

Activity-based learning

No rote learning

International curriculum

Principal's message

Dr. Parvinder Kaur

Principal, Smart Start – The Premium School

Dr. Parvinder Kaur leads Smart Start with the heart of a mother and the vision of an educator. Her mission is to give every child a beautiful childhood and every parent a partner they can trust.

Dear Parents,

I am Dr. Parvinder, Principal - Smart Start The Premium School.

Today, I am not speaking to you as a Principal, I am speaking to you as a mother.

When you leave your little one at our door, with that small bag and teary eyes - I understand that feeling. Because for you, your child is your whole world. And from that moment, your world becomes our responsibility.

At Smart Start, our vision was never just to be a school that teaches A-B-C. My vision has always been to create a second home. A home where your child is not just taught, but nourished. Where we nurture not only their mind, but their heart. Where we teach them not only how to hold a pencil, but how to hold confidence. Not only how to speak English, but how to speak with respect.

My two promises to you: 1. We will nourish your child. Every child will get personal love, personal attention, and personal care. We will work on their confidence, their communication, their values, and their happiness. Because marks can be improved later, but childhood confidence, once lost, never comes back. 2. We will build a bond with you. I don't want you to be just a parent who drops and picks up the child. I want you to be my partner. My door is always open for you. Your worries will be my worries.

I am not here to give your child just formal schooling. I am here to give them a beautiful childhood that they will cherish forever.

Come, let's build their future together, with love.

Warmly,

Dr. Parvinder, Principal
