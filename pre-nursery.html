About Our Pre-Nursery Wing

At SMART START – The Premium School, our Pre-Nursery Wing is designed as the first step towards academics.

This wing is specially made to prepare your child for Nursery. That is why it is called Pre-Nursery – a gentle bridge between play-based learning and formal learning.

Curriculum & Care

What We Focus On In Our Pre-Nursery

Thoughtfully crafted learning milestones designed for ages 2.5–3 years.

1

Introduction to Pencil

In this wing, we introduce the pencil for the first time. As per NEP guidelines and medical advice, a child should hold a pencil only after the age of 2.5 years, when their tiny fingers and muscles are ready. We follow this strictly and teach the correct pencil grip in a fun and pressure-free way.

2

Writing in Four Lines

Children learn to write in four lines, which helps them understand line discipline, letter formation and neat writing from the very beginning.

3

Preparation for Nursery

All activities in Pre-Nursery are planned to make children ready for Nursery – so they enter the next class with confidence, not fear.

4

Enhanced Communication Skills

Through daily conversations, storytelling, rhymes and group activities, children learn to speak clearly and express their thoughts better.

5

Stronger Recognition

Children strengthen their recognition of alphabets, numbers, colours, shapes and objects, and begin to connect them with the real world.

6

Etiquette & Good Habits

We continue to nurture etiquette and good habits so children grow into polite, well-mannered learners.

Our Promise

A Loving Promise to Every Pre-Nursery Parent

In our Pre-Nursery, your child takes their first confident step towards academics – with a pencil in hand, a smile on face, and a heart ready to learn.

Because at SMART START, we prepare children not just for the next class, but for a lifetime of learning.
