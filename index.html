Our approach

Eight ways to be SMART

Every child is SMART in a different way. Our Multiple Intelligence approach discovers each child's strengths and builds on them through play. Tap an intelligence to explore.

Linguistic Intelligence

Word Wizards

The love of words, stories, rhymes and conversation. Linguistic learners talk, listen and imagine in language.

  • Storytelling and show-and-tell
  • Rhymes and phonics games
  • Interactive alphabet books
  • Speaking on stage
Logical-Mathematical Intelligence

Little Problem Solvers

Curiosity about patterns, numbers and how things work. These children love sorting, counting and asking why.

  • Stacking, sorting and matching
  • Counting with real objects
  • Pattern and shape games
  • Simple puzzles
Spatial Intelligence

Picture Thinkers

Thinking in pictures, colours and shapes. Spatial learners imagine, draw and build.

  • Finger painting and drawing
  • Building with blocks
  • Colour and shape hunts
  • Theme-based craft
Bodily-Kinesthetic Intelligence

Moving Mountains

Learning through movement and touch. These children climb, jump, dance and learn best when their bodies are involved.

  • Climbing and ball-pit play
  • Hoop and obstacle games
  • Dance and martial-arts demonstrations
  • Role play and costumes
Musical Intelligence

Rhythm Makers

Sensitivity to rhythm, melody and sound. Musical children hum, tap and move to the beat.

  • Songs and action rhymes
  • Dhol, dandiya and folk dance
  • Rhythm clapping games
  • Stage performances
Interpersonal Intelligence

Friendship Builders

Understanding and connecting with others. These children make friends easily, share and lead.

  • Circle time and group play
  • Festival celebrations together
  • Sharing and turn-taking games
  • Team activities
Intrapersonal Intelligence

Inner Stars

Knowing one's own feelings and strengths. Intrapersonal children are thoughtful, independent and self-aware.

  • Quiet focus time
  • Feelings and mood check-ins
  • Independent worksheets
  • Goal-setting through stickers
Naturalistic Intelligence

Nature Explorers

A bond with plants, animals and the natural world. These children notice, collect and care for living things.

  • Planting and watering
  • Animal and jungle themes
  • Outdoor play on the grass
  • Seasons and weather talk
